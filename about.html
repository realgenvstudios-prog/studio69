About

Made for your space.
Made to last.

Studio 69 designs and builds in solid timber — doors, furniture, kitchens and custom pieces.

Wood has a way of making a space feel different. At Studio 69, we work with solid timber selected for its character, strength and natural beauty. From doors and furniture to kitchens and custom pieces.

We choose the right timber for the piece, then shape, finish and build it with care. The result is something made for your space, made to last, and made to feel like it belongs there.
