Wood has a way of making a space feel different. At Studio 69, we work with solid timber selected for its character, strength and natural beauty. From doors and furniture to kitchens and custom pieces.

We choose the right timber for the piece, then shape, finish and build it with care. The result is something made for your space, made to last, and made to feel like it belongs there.

Order Process

A journey of inspiration and craftsmanship: the timeline of your door order from beginning to end

01

Tell Us What You're Imagining

It starts with an idea. A drawing, a photograph, a reference you've saved, or simply something you've had in mind for your space. Tell us what you're looking to create, and we'll help turn the idea into a clear direction.

02

We Make It Real On Paper

Once we understand what you're after, we work through the dimensions, materials, details and finishes. Your design is refined into something precise, practical and ready to build with you involved before anything is made.

03

Made By Hand. Built To Last.

This is where the idea leaves the screen. Our workshop turns selected materials into the finished piece, with every cut, joint, edge and finish carefully considered.

04

Finished. Delivered. Installed.

When it's ready, we bring it to its final destination. Our team handles the installation and the finishing touches, so what started as an idea becomes part of your space.
